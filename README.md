# RP-25

The RP-25 Radial Pitch Mapper is a browser-based utility designed to generate precise reference tones for tuning unquantised analogue sequencers and synthesisers.

**Core Features**

*   **Radial Interface:** A central rotary knob mapped across a 270-degree sweep, spanning a 25-note range (two full octaves).
*   **Direct Key Access:** Clickable notes arranged around the dial allow for instant pitch selection without manual sweeping.
*   **Hold (Drone) Mode:** Sustains the audio indefinitely, freeing your hands to adjust hardware dials while listening for phase beating.
*   **Waveform Selection:** Switch between a sawtooth wave (rich in harmonics to highlight tuning discrepancies) and a triangle wave (for matching purer fundamental tones).
*   **Octave Shifting:** Shift the central pitch down two octaves or up three octaves to match your hardware's operating range.
*   **Visual Feedback:** A CRT-style display shows the current note and its exact frequency in Hz.

**Recommended Tuning Workflow**

1. Connect your analogue sequencer to a hardware synthesiser oscillator.
2. Select your target note on the RP-25 and activate the **Hold** function.
3. Match the RP-25's waveform (Saw or Tri) and octave to your hardware synth.
4. Adjust the sequencer's unquantised voltage knob until the physical synth's pitch matches the RP-25, listening carefully until the auditory beating stops.
